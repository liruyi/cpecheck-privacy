# Privacy Policy — CPE Check

> 托管后把 URL 填进 App Store Connect 的 Privacy Policy 字段。
> App Store 隐私标签选择：**Data Not Collected**（无需申报任何数据类型）。

**Effective date: October 15, 2026**

CPE Check ("the App") is designed to work entirely on your device.

## Data We Collect
**None.** The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no server.

## Data Stored on Your Device
- The certification, renewal dates, and CPE activities you enter are stored **only** in your device's local storage.
- Deleting the App (or using "Remove credential" in the App) deletes this data permanently. We cannot recover it.

## Notifications
The App sends local reminders about your renewal deadline. These are generated by iOS on your device. Enabling them is optional.

## Third Parties
The App links to public ISC2 web pages for rule sources. Tapping a link opens Safari/your browser; standard browser policies apply. The App itself sends no data.

## Children
The App is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children under 17.
